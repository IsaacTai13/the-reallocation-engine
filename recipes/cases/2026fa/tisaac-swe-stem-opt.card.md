# Recipe: SWE STEM-OPT Runway Finder

**Who it's for:** F-1 STEM Information Systems students (pre-OPT) targeting broader Software Engineering, Development, DevOps, and Test roles.
**What it does:** Filters out jobs that explicitly ban international students or match user-excluded specializations (e.g., Data, ML). Scores remaining jobs based on H-1B sponsorship history, high salary, AI-resilience (O*NET), recent funding (SEC Form D), location (Boston/major hubs), and work model (Remote/Hybrid).
**What it decides:** 
- **Apply / Consider / Skip** based on the final score.
- **-1 (Error):** Assigns a -1 score with a reason log if a specific job encounters an unparseable error, without crashing the whole script.
